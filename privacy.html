Privacy Policy

Painted Sagas Publisher · last updated 8 November 2026

Painted Sagas Publisher ("the app") is a private tool used only by the operator of The Painted Sagas to manage the operator's own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It is not available to the public.

Which data the app accesses

  • With the operator's permission, through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API: uploading the operator's own videos, and reading the operator's own videos, their statistics (for example views, watch time, likes) and the public comments on them.
  • Through the Instagram API: publishing the operator's own posts and reading their statistics and public comments.

How the data is used and stored

  • Statistics are stored on the operator's own server to compare episodes and posting times.
  • Comment texts are stored without usernames; mentions of other accounts are removed. They are used only to summarise how the stories are received.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the operator's server and are never shared.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. On request, stored statistics and comment texts are deleted.
