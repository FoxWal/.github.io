The Painted Sagas

An anthology of short, painted fantasy stories.

The Painted Sagas publishes fictional, AI-generated fantasy series as short videos on Instagram (@the.painted.sagas) and YouTube (@thepaintedsagas). All characters and worlds are fictional, and every post is labelled as AI-generated.

Painted Sagas Publisher

Painted Sagas Publisher is a private tool used only by the operator of these two accounts. It uploads our own videos to our own channel and reads the statistics and public comments of our own videos, so we can learn which stories and posting times work best. It is not offered to anyone else.
